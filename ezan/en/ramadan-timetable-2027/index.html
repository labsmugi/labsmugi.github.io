The Adhan Time app works out the prayer times where you are, offline, and reminds you with the adhan; it also has a qibla compass, the Quran, a Ramadan timetable and sehri reminders. Free, with no ads.

App StoreGoogle Play

Times are calculated with each country's usual method, as in the app: Karachi (University of Islamic Sciences) with Hanafi Asr in Pakistan, ISNA in the US and Canada and the Muslim World League elsewhere; where twilight never ends in summer, Fajr and Isha follow the angle-based rule. Local mosque timetables can differ by a few minutes.
