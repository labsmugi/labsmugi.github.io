How to play

Wordmill is a free word game with new puzzles every day: three word ladders (easy, medium and hard), a mill of seven letters and a proverb hidden behind numbers. Everyone gets the same puzzles on the same day, and you can share your result without giving away a letter. Nothing is translated: every language has its own words and its own proverbs.

🪜 Ladder

Get from the start word to the target. Tap a letter, then pick a new one: each step must be a real word. Reach it in the fewest steps for three stars. Green marks show letters already in the right place.

🌀 Mill

Tap the letters on the wheel to build words of three letters or more. Each letter can be used once per word. Find a word with all seven letters for the mill word.

🔐 Cipher

Each number hides one letter, the same everywhere. Tap a square and type the letter you think it is. Solve the whole proverb with as few mistakes as you can.
